## Annotation

This Army field manual covers Holistic Health and Fitness and places physical readiness, sleep, and nutrition in one system so sleep, activity, and nutrition are readiness behaviors rather than separate hobbies. It covers the institutional backbone behind this collection’s Performance Triad framing. It is valuable because it ties duty and performance to the same three pillars civilians often split into “gym tips,” “diet tips,” and “sleep hacks.” While this source does not focus on training after 55, athletes under occupational demand after 55—and leaders who model capacity—will still benefit from the core concepts contained within the Field Manual. However, the FM assumes military-level resources and may not fully apply to civilians with limited support — adapt the principles to what you actually have. Pair midlife physiology with [[A-Introduction/S32-endo-anabolic-aging|Endo]] and [[A-Introduction/S33-obryan-bone-strength|O’Bryan]], the sleep pillar with [[E-Sleep-and-recovery/S13-walsh-athlete-sleep|Walsh]], the training pillar with [[C-Exercise/index|Exercise]], fuel with [[D-Nutrition/index|Nutrition]], and recovery habits with [American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)](https://www.acsm.org) · [[E-Sleep-and-recovery/S21-acsm-recovery-older-adults|recovery guidance]].
