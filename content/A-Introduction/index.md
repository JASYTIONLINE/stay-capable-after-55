# A. Introduction (Don't believe everything you read on the internet)

The concept of the Triad of health and fitness came from the Army **Holistic Health and Fitness (FM 7-22)** treats sleep, activity, and nutrition as **one readiness system** — not three separate disciplines. This collection introduces the concept as Army Doctrine, then expands the research to include civilian insight and research into a broader approach to the journey of getting and staying healthy over the age of 55 while maintaining a demanding and active lifestyle. 

Included in this section are some tools to help you create and curate your own collection of research materials, regardless of the subject. The other tools here teach you how to judge source material so you do not confuse marketing with evidence..

What is holistic readiness, and how do you tell a useful source from a look-alike? That is what these four sources answer.
