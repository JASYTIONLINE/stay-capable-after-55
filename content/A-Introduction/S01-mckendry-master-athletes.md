# S01 — McKendry et al. (2018)

**Category:** [[A-Introduction/index|A — Introduction]]
**Type:** Scholarly

## Citation

McKendry, J., Breen, L., Shad, B. J., & Greig, C. A. (2018). Muscle morphology and performance in master athletes: A systematic review and meta-analyses. *Ageing Research Reviews, 45*, 62–82. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.arr.2018.04.007

**Link:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29715523/

## Annotation

This source sits in Introduction **not only for its findings** on master-athlete muscle and performance, but because it is a **worked example** of research that studied people who kept training — the kind of paper you want to recognize after you use tools like the [[A-Introduction/S16-craap-test|CRAAP test]] and [[A-Introduction/S17-adler-how-to-read|Adler’s framework]]. The peer-reviewed systematic review and meta-analyses examines chronically trained master athletes and how long-term training relates to muscle size, structure, and performance across training modes. It covers evidence drawn from people who kept training, not only from sedentary older adults. Athletes after 55, coaches, and readers learning to judge fitness research will get the most from it. It offers little detail on combat or tactical sports, so pair practice-based martial arts sources under [[C-Exercise/index|Exercise]] when that context matters. Strength and dose questions that follow belong in [[C-Exercise/index|Exercise]] — start with [[C-Exercise/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]] and [[C-Exercise/S02-borde-dose-response|Borde]].
