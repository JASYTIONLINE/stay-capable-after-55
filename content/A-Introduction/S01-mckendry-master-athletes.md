**Category:** [[A-Introduction/index|A — Introduction]]
**Type:** Scholarly

## Citation

McKendry, J., Breen, L., Shad, B. J., & Greig, C. A. (2018). Muscle morphology and performance in master athletes: A systematic review and meta-analyses. *Ageing Research Reviews, 45*, 62–82. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.arr.2018.04.007

**Link:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29715523/

## Annotation

This source sits in Introduction as a **worked example** of research that studied people who kept training—not sedentary-only samples. The peer-reviewed systematic review and meta-analyses examine chronically trained master athletes and how long-term training relates to muscle size, structure, and performance across training modes. It covers evidence drawn from people who kept training well into their 50s and beyond. Athletes after 55 and coaches who need still-training research will get the most from it. It offers little detail on combat or tactical sports, so pair practice-based martial arts sources under [[C-Exercise/index|Exercise]] when that context matters. Strength and dose questions that follow belong in [[C-Exercise/index|Exercise]] — start with [[C-Exercise/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]] and [[C-Exercise/S02-borde-dose-response|Borde]]. Pair with [[A-Introduction/S32-endo-anabolic-aging|Endo]] and [[A-Introduction/S33-obryan-bone-strength|O’Bryan]] for midlife physiology beside still-training evidence.
