## Step 3: Reflection

The strongest parts of this draft is the structure (the A-to-F path) and the audience lock, which allows the reader to journey through the material. Introduction and Takeaways already carry the same claim: adapt the lifestyle to the aging body without quitting a demanding life. We can't continue to do what worked yesterday tomorrow. As we age, our body changes and we must adjust our system. Clearance-is-not-readiness, anabolic stall, and volume honesty recur across sections, so the product reads like one system rather than five topic pages.

What still needs work is polish. Everything still reads like notes instead of engaging the reader. I will flesh the knee story so the comeback stake feels earned, audit attributions so every in-prose cite matches G, and sharpen the practical beats in Returning through Sleep so “what to change first” is unmistakable.  I am also unsure of how to set up the transition, the current up/down link system is unclear. I will work on how to make the pages flow into one and another intuitively. Priority for Week 08: personal detail, citation audit, then tighten any thin passages before Project 02 final submission.
