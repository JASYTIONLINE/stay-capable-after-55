# A. Evidence filter

This room is not only a pile of fitness facts. It is here to **teach you how to judge sources** — and to show **good examples** of research that fits people who still train after 55.

Some items (like the CRAAP test and Adler) are tools you can use on *any* topic. Others (like McKendry on master athletes) are included **not only for what they say**, but because they are a clear example of evidence built on people who kept training — the kind of source that should pass your filter when you are sorting look-alike “senior fitness” advice.

Use these tools on anything you find later, then click a title below for the full annotation. Coming back after time off? Start in [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/index|Returning to an active lifestyle]]. Ready to program training? Go to [[C-Programming/index|Programming]].
