## Introduction

*(Draft — expand to 200–300 words before Project 01 submit. Cover purpose, who this is for and who it is not, scope in and out, and how to use the categories below.)*

This collection is a trail through strength and conditioning guidance for people who still train with purpose after 55—sport, martial arts, tactical or occupational fitness, or serious recreational athletics. It is not a generic senior fitness list.

Sources are organized so a first-time visitor can browse by the question they have: what evidence applies to still-training bodies, how to program, how to manage load and recovery, how nutrition supports capacity, and who has lived this path as a practitioner.
