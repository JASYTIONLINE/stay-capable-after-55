# D. Nutrition - Fuel for the work

This is the nutrition pillar of the triad from [[A-Introduction/index|Introduction]] and [FM 7-22](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN44522-FM_7-22-002-WEB-7.pdf)<sup>[[G-Sources/S15-fm-7-22-h2f|S15]]</sup>. My framing: fuel for capacity under training, not a second diet collection.

## Protein serves the training

For older athletes who still train, protein intake, timing, and sex-aware considerations matter for keeping muscle while supporting the work. Desbrow and colleagues review that territory directly<sup>[[G-Sources/S11-desbrow-older-athletes|S11]]</sup> ([MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/13/5/1409)). It belongs beside the activity plan in [[C-Exercise/index|Exercise]], especially when anabolic response is blunted after midlife (Endo<sup>[[G-Sources/S32-endo-anabolic-aging|S32]]</sup>).

Broader protein and exercise dosing standards sit in the [International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN)](https://www.issn.org) position stand<sup>[[G-Sources/S28-issn-protein-stand|S28]]</sup>. Apply older-athlete adjustments from Desbrow rather than copying a 25-year-old’s template.

## Cutting without throwing away muscle

If you cut weight, protect fat-free mass. Verreijen and colleagues tested high protein with or without resistance training during weight loss in adults 55-80<sup>[[G-Sources/S12-verreijen-protein-weight-loss|S12]]</sup> ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-017-0229-6)). That combination of fuel and lifting matches how I treat capacity in this primer. My rule: do not let a diet-only plan erase the strength work you need for the mat or the job.

Overnight timing is one lever among many. Pre-sleep protein research (Trommelen and van Loon<sup>[[G-Sources/S14-trommelen-presleep-protein|S14]]</sup>) ties the sleep window to training adaptations, which is why [[E-Sleep-and-recovery/index|Sleep and recovery]] and nutrition belong in the same readiness system.

## Keep it practical

Plate-method coaching can help when you need a simple structure. Use practical media carefully and verify targets against Desbrow and ISSN (Elevate Your Plate<sup>[[G-Sources/S29-body-signals-elevate-plate|S29]]</sup>). My take: commercial gadgets are optional. The work and the protein are not.
