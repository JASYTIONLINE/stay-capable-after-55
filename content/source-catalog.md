## Doctrine lens (FM 7-22 / Performance Triad)

**Core message:** Holistic Health and Fitness treats **sleep, activity, and nutrition as one readiness system**. More training alone is not more ready.

**Labels:** Reinforces · Agrees (different frame) · Complements · Orthogonal · Tension

No live source outright contradicts the triad. Tensions are mostly single-pillar emphases that need the rest of the path.
