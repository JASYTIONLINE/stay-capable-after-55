## Annotation

This *Sleep Is A Skill* episode features Dr. Jeffrey Durmer — neurologist, sleep physician, and sleep performance director linked to USA Olympic Weightlifting — on sleep care for peak performance. It covers high-performance sleep as a trainable system, not a vague wellness tip, which matches athletes who still train under real demand. It is valuable as the named podcast footprint for this collection and as accessible audio beside denser science. Listeners after 55 managing occupational schedules and hard training will benefit most. Host framing is consumer/practitioner media, so verify clinical claims against [[D-Load-and-recovery/S13-walsh-athlete-sleep|Walsh’s BJSM consensus]] and keep doctrine context from [[D-Load-and-recovery/S15-fm-7-22-h2f|FM 7-22]].
