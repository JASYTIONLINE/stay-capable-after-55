## Annotation

This Army field manual covers Holistic Health and Fitness and places physical readiness, sleep, and nutrition in one system so sleep, activity, and nutrition are readiness behaviors rather than separate hobbies. It covers the institutional backbone behind this collection’s Performance Triad framing. It is valuable because it ties duty and performance to the same three pillars civilians often split into “gym tips,” “diet tips,” and “sleep hacks.” Athletes under occupational demand after 55, and leaders who model capacity, will benefit most. It assumes military-level resources and may not fully apply to civilians with limited support — adapt the principles to what you actually have. Screen civilian spin-offs of “H2F” marketing with the [[A-Evidence-filter/S16-craap-test|CRAAP test]]. Pair the sleep pillar with [[D-Load-and-recovery/S13-walsh-athlete-sleep|Walsh]] and [[D-Load-and-recovery/S22-sleep-is-a-skill-durmer|Sleep Is A Skill (Durmer)]], the training pillar with [[C-Programming/index|Programming]], fuel with [[E-Nutrition-support/index|Nutrition support]], and the short visual message with [[D-Load-and-recovery/S20-dvids-h2f-video|the DVIDS H2F video]].
