## Annotation

This official Defense Media video introduces Army Holistic Health and Fitness teams and the idea that readiness combines physical fitness with broader health domains to prevent injury and improve performance. It covers a short institutional statement of the same holistic logic behind [[D-Load-and-recovery/S15-fm-7-22-h2f|FM 7-22]] and the Performance Triad. It is valuable as a plain visual for military and occupational athletes who need to see why sleep, training, and nutrition sit together — useful after you practice judging media with the [[A-Evidence-filter/S16-craap-test|CRAAP test]] (authority and purpose are clear here; depth is not). It is a brief PSA, not a programming course. Pair with FM 7-22 for doctrine, [[D-Load-and-recovery/S13-walsh-athlete-sleep|Walsh]] and [[E-Nutrition-support/index|Nutrition support]] for civilian evidence depth, and [[C-Programming/index|Programming]] when you translate the message into weekly training decisions.
