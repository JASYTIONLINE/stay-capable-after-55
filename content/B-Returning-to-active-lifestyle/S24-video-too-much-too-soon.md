# S24 — Too much too soon (video)

**Category:** [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/index|B — Returning to an active lifestyle]]
**Type:** Media (video)

## Citation

Runner’s World–style discussion. (n.d.). *Cleared to Run Again After an Injury? This 9-Step Plan Helps You Come Back Stronger Than Ever.* [Video]. YouTube. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OByODrdaQKM

## Annotation

This conversation names the comeback failure mode athletes know too well: **too much too soon** — returning to old pace, old weekly volume, or old session length because memory outruns tissue capacity. It covers a phased return-to-run mindset instead of a calendar fantasy. It is valuable as a dramatized mirror for anyone who got hurt by running too far, too soon, or on terrain they had not earned yet after a long break. Midlife athletes rebuilding an active lifestyle will benefit most. It is popular fitness media; triangulate the principle with [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S27-acsm-preparticipation-prep|ACSM’s “start low and go slow”]] and [[A-Triad-and-judgment/S17-adler-how-to-read|Adler]] before treating any numbered plan as gospel. Watch beside [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S23-video-cleared-not-ready|cleared ≠ ready]]. When ego whispers old PRs, answer with [[E-Sleep-and-recovery/S07-sullivan-masters-volume|Sullivan on masters volume]].
