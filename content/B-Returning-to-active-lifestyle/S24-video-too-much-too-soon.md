## Citation

Runner’s World–style discussion. (n.d.). *Cleared to Run Again After an Injury? This 9-Step Plan Helps You Come Back Stronger Than Ever.* [Video]. YouTube. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OByODrdaQKM

## Annotation

This conversation names the comeback failure mode athletes know too well: **too much too soon** — returning to old pace, old weekly volume, or old session length because memory outruns tissue capacity. It covers a phased return-to-run mindset instead of a calendar fantasy. It is valuable as a dramatized mirror for anyone who got hurt by running too far, too soon, or on terrain they had not earned yet after a long break. This happened to me right after my promotion to First Sergeant (1SG), putting my new job and my entire career in jeopardy. Midlife athletes rebuilding an active lifestyle will benefit most. It is popular fitness media; triangulate the principle with [American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)](https://www.acsm.org) · [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S27-acsm-preparticipation-prep|“start low and go slow”]] and [[A-Introduction/S17-adler-how-to-read|Adler]] before treating any numbered plan as gospel. Watch beside [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S23-video-cleared-not-ready|cleared ≠ ready]]. When ego whispers old personal records (PRs), answer with [[E-Sleep-and-recovery/S07-sullivan-masters-volume|Sullivan on masters volume]].
