# S25 — Gradual return after time off (video)

**Category:** [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/index|B — Returning to an active lifestyle]]

## Citation

[Physical therapy / return-to-run guidance]. (n.d.). *Post Injury Return to Running Program* [Video]. YouTube. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1t2PDmJyCAM

## Annotation

This video stresses a blunt rule of return: **the longer you have been off, the slower and more gradual the rebuild must be** — not only for the injured part, but for the whole body that deconditioned with it. It covers pain-guided progression and patience as preparation, not as fear. It is valuable as the third motivator in the set: clearance and enthusiasm still lose to tissue timelines. Athletes after 55 coming back from months or years away from hard training will benefit most. It is clinician/coach media, so verify claims with [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S27-acsm-preparticipation-prep|ACSM progressive preparation]] and keep sleep/recovery honest via [[D-Load-and-recovery/S13-walsh-athlete-sleep|Walsh]]. Use with [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S23-video-cleared-not-ready|cleared ≠ ready]] and [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S24-video-too-much-too-soon|too much too soon]] as the emotional spine of this shelf before you touch [[C-Programming/index|Programming]].
