## Annotation

This video drives one hard lesson: a clinician clearing you to move is not the same as being prepared for sport-level stress. It covers why “pain is better” or “the doctor said I’m fine” still leaves you short on strength, impact tolerance, and progressive loading — the gap that reinjures athletes who rush back. It is valuable as this section’s motivator for former high performers who remember old standards and try to skip the rebuild. Athletes after 55 returning from layoff, injury, or years of inconsistent training will feel the warning most. It is popular coaching media, not a peer-reviewed trial, so run the [Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose (CRAAP)](https://library.csuchico.edu/sites/default/files/craap-test.pdf) [[A-Introduction/S16-craap-test|test]] on authority and purpose, then lock the principle with [American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)](https://www.acsm.org) [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S27-acsm-preparticipation-prep|progressive transitional guidance]]. Pair with [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/S24-video-too-much-too-soon|too much too soon]], then earn your way into [[C-Exercise/index|Exercise]].
