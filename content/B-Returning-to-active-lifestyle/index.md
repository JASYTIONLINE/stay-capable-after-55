# B. Returning to an active lifestyle

**Purpose:** Show how to come back after time off or injury without getting hurt by old ego intensity.

This section is primer content, not a source shelf. Full citations live in [[G-Sources/index|G. Sources]].

## Outline (draft)

- The comeback failure mode: memory of the old self outruns tissue capacity
- Clearance ≠ readiness (medical OK is not sport-level preparation)
- Too much too soon: ego volume after layoff
- Unaccustomed hard effort raises short-term cardiac risk, especially if you train infrequently
- Progressive prep: start low, go slow, earn intensity before chasing old personal records
- Hand-off: once you have a base, train hard enough on purpose → [[C-Exercise/index|Exercise]]
