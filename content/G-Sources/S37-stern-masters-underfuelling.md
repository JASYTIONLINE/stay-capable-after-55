# S37 — Stern (masters under-fuelling)

**Category:** [[G-Sources/index|G — Sources]]
**Supports:** D
**Type:** Professional / practitioner

## Citation

Stern, R. (n.d.). Masters cyclists: You’re under-fuelling, and it’s costing you more than you think. *Road Bike Rider*. https://www.roadbikerider.com/masters-cyclists-under-fuelling/

**Coach site:** https://www.cyclecoach.com/

## Annotation

This coaching article for masters cyclists names a common failure mode: cutting fuel to get lighter for hills, then training and racing flat because energy and carbohydrate no longer match the work. Stern has coached since 1997, including club racers through Tour de France professionals. The piece covers matching carbohydrate to training load, protein for aging athletes, and energy availability for performance and bone, and it points readers at review evidence such as [[G-Sources/S11-strasser-older-athletes|Strasser et al. (2021)]]. Masters athletes who still train hard while quietly under-eating will benefit most. It is practitioner writing, not a randomized trial, so treat the lived pattern as coaching insight and keep hard numbers grounded in [[G-Sources/S11-strasser-older-athletes|Strasser]] and [[G-Sources/S34-issn-diets-body-composition|ISSN diets and body composition]].
