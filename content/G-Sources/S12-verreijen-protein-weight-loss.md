## Citation

Verreijen, A. M., Engberink, M. F., Memelink, R. G., van der Plas, S. E., Visser, M., & Weijs, P. J. M. (2017). Effect of a high protein diet and/or resistance exercise on the preservation of fat free mass during weight loss in overweight and obese older adults: A randomized controlled trial. *Nutrition Journal, 16*, 10. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-017-0229-6

**Link:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-017-0229-6

## Annotation

This randomized trial examines high protein intake with or without resistance training (RT) during weight loss in adults 55–80, with a focus on fat-free mass preservation. It covers the lived problem of cutting weight under training load without throwing away muscle. It is valuable because it pairs nutrition and lifting in one design — the same combination this collection treats as capacity support. People cutting fat after midlife while still training will benefit most. The sample is overweight/obese adults, so apply carefully to already-lean masters athletes. Frame results with [[G-Sources/S11-strasser-older-athletes|Strasser]] and [[G-Sources/S34-issn-diets-body-composition|ISSN diets and body composition]], and keep the training side honest with [[G-Sources/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]]. Practical plate-method coaching sits in [[G-Sources/S29-body-signals-elevate-plate|Elevate Your Plate]]; micronutrient caution lives in [[G-Sources/S36-nih-ods-nutrient-recs|NIH ODS]]. Do not let commercial diet pitches skip this evidence.
