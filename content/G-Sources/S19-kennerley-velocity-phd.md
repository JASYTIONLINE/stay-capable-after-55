## Annotation

This Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) thesis examines maximal-intentional velocity (power-focused) resistance training design and implementation for older adults. It covers how to build and study training that emphasizes intent to move with speed — the kind of power work that [[G-Sources/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]] and current [American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)](https://www.acsm.org) guidance flag as important for function after midlife. It is valuable as deep scholarly work beyond a position-statement paragraph: methods, design choices, and older-adult application in one document. Coaches and serious athletes after 55 interested in power methods will benefit most. Thesis length and academic framing are limitations for casual readers; use selected findings, not the whole PDF as a consumer program. Hear the guideline layer in [[G-Sources/S18-currier-acsm-video|Currier’s ACSM interview]], then come here when you need depth.
