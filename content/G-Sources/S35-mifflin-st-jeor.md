# S35 — Mifflin et al. (1990) resting energy equation

**Category:** [[G-Sources/index|G — Sources]]
**Supports:** D
**Type:** Scholarly

## Citation

Mifflin, M. D., St Jeor, S. T., Hill, L. A., Scott, B. J., Daugherty, S. A., & Koh, Y. O. (1990). A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals. *The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 51*(2), 241–247. https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/51.2.241

**Link:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/

## Annotation

This classic paper derives the Mifflin–St Jeor equations widely used to estimate resting metabolic rate from weight, height, age, and sex before activity factors are applied. It covers a teachable starting point for calorie planning, not a substitute for measured metabolism. It is valuable because [[D-Nutrition/index|Nutrition]] needs a citable method for “how do I estimate fuel needs?” while still sending readers to clinical judgment and modern planners. Athletes after 55 should treat the output as an estimate and refine from weight, training, and energy feedback. Pair goal adjustments with [[G-Sources/S34-issn-diets-body-composition|ISSN diets and body composition]] and midlife protein context with [[G-Sources/S11-strasser-older-athletes|Strasser]]. Practical calculators that implement related planning logic include the [NIH Body Weight Planner](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/bwp).
