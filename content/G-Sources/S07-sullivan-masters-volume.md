# S07 — Sullivan & Baker (masters volume)

**Category:** [[G-Sources/index|G — Sources]]
**Supports:** E · F
**Type:** Professional / practitioner

## Citation

Sullivan, J., & Baker, A. (n.d.). Volume and the masters lifter. *Starting Strength*. https://startingstrength.com/article/volume-and-the-masters-lifter

**PDF:** https://startingstrength.com/contentfiles/volume_and_the_masters_lifter.pdf

## Annotation

This practitioner article argues that many masters lifters are volume-sensitive: more sets and frequency often produce soreness and stalls rather than progress. It covers a failure mode athletes after 50 recognize in their own logs — adding work when recovery is already behind. It is valuable as a coaching voice that names load mismanagement without pretending to be a randomized trial. Lifters after midlife who keep adding volume and stop recovering will benefit most. It is not peer-reviewed and sits inside a Starting Strength methodological tradition, so triangulate with [[G-Sources/S02-borde-dose-response|Borde]], [[G-Sources/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]], and treat individual variation as large. Pair with [[G-Sources/S13-walsh-athlete-sleep|Walsh]] when under-recovery is really sleep debt, and with [[G-Sources/S03-markov-concurrent|Markov]] when conditioning volume is the silent pile-on.
