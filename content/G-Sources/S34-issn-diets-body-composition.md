## Annotation

This ISSN position stand reviews how energy surplus, energy deficit, and macronutrient distribution interact with fat loss and lean-mass goals in exercising people. It covers the “goal first, then calories and macros” logic needed to turn nutrition into a training support system rather than a trend diet. It is valuable as the body-composition planning spine beside the protein-specific ISSN stand. Athletes after 55 choosing among fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance will benefit most when they also apply older-athlete protein and micronutrient framing from [[G-Sources/S11-strasser-older-athletes|Strasser]] and protein dosing from [[G-Sources/S28-issn-protein-stand|ISSN protein]]. It is not a meal-plan app and not midlife-specific by itself.
