# E. Nutrition support

This shelf is **fuel for capacity**, not a second diet collection. Protein, intake structure, and timing support lifting, recovery, and staying capable after 55 — especially when cutting weight without throwing away muscle.

Judge nutrition claims with [[A-Evidence-filter/index|Evidence filter]]. If you are coming back after time off, prepare first in [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/index|Returning to an active lifestyle]]. Pair session fuel with sleep and recovery in [[D-Load-and-recovery/index|Load and recovery]] and the training plan in [[C-Programming/index|Programming]].
