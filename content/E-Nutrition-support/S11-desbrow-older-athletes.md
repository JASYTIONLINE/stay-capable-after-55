**Category:** [[E-Nutrition-support/index|E — Nutrition support]]

## Citation

Desbrow, B., Burd, N. A., Tarnopolsky, M., Moore, D. R., & Cox, G. R. (2021). Nutrition for older athletes: Focus on sex-differences. *Nutrients, 13*(5), 1409. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13051409

**Link:** https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/13/5/1409

## Annotation

This peer-reviewed nutrition review is written for older athletes and covers protein for training, higher protein during energy restriction to protect lean mass while losing fat, and sex differences in nutritional needs. It covers diet as fuel for people who still train — not frail-senior meal advice. It is valuable as the scholarly cornerstone of this shelf and a check against diet-collection drift. Active athletes after 55 who may be cutting fat while trying to keep muscle will benefit most. It is a broad sports-nutrition review rather than a complete meal plan, so pair practical intake structure with [[E-Nutrition-support/S29-body-signals-elevate-plate|Elevate Your Plate]] and protein standards with [[E-Nutrition-support/S28-issn-protein-stand|ISSN]]. Use [[A-Evidence-filter/S16-craap-test|CRAAP]] on flashy “anti-aging diet” media before they outrank this paper. Connect overnight strategy to [[E-Nutrition-support/S14-trommelen-presleep-protein|Trommelen]] and weight-cut evidence to [[E-Nutrition-support/S12-verreijen-protein-weight-loss|Verreijen]].
