## Annotation

This GSSI article asks whether master athletes need more protein than younger athletes and explains anabolic resistance in aging muscle versus the effect of high training volumes. It covers masters-specific protein framing for people who still train. It is valuable as readable professional synthesis between sedentary older-adult guidelines and athlete fueling. Masters athletes and coaches setting intake for training and muscle building will benefit most. Industry sponsorship is a limitation — cross-check numbers with [[E-Nutrition-support/S28-issn-protein-stand|ISSN]] and [[E-Nutrition-support/S11-desbrow-older-athletes|Desbrow]], and judge purpose with [[A-Evidence-filter/S16-craap-test|CRAAP]]. Evidence specific to masters remains thinner than youth literature, so treat recommendations as informed guidance ([[A-Evidence-filter/S17-adler-how-to-read|Adler]]: incomplete vs wrong). Pair weight-cut contexts with [[E-Nutrition-support/S12-verreijen-protein-weight-loss|Verreijen]].
