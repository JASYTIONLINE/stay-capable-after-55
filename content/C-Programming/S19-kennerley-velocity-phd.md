## Annotation

This PhD thesis examines maximal-intentional velocity (power-focused) resistance training design and implementation for older adults. It covers how to build and study training that emphasizes intent to move with speed — the kind of power work [[C-Programming/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]] and current ACSM guidance flag as important for function after midlife. It is valuable as deep scholarly work beyond a position-statement paragraph: methods, design choices, and older-adult application in one document. Coaches and serious athletes after 55 interested in power methods will benefit most. Thesis length and academic framing are limitations for casual readers; use selected findings, not the whole PDF as a consumer program. Screen any popular “power for seniors” video with [[A-Evidence-filter/S16-craap-test|CRAAP]] and [[A-Evidence-filter/S17-adler-how-to-read|Adler]], hear the guideline layer in [[C-Programming/S18-currier-acsm-video|Currier’s ACSM interview]], then come here when you need depth. Pair dose questions with [[C-Programming/S02-borde-dose-response|Borde]] and recovery limits with [[D-Load-and-recovery/index|Load and recovery]].
