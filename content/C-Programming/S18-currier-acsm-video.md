## Annotation

This long-form interview features Dr. Brad Currier, PhD, a lead author on the ACSM’s updated resistance-training position stand, explaining evidence-based prescription for healthy adults, including power-related points that matter for function after midlife. It covers guideline ideas in spoken form: consistency, effective loading, and why complexity is not the same as quality. It is valuable because it translates a major professional update through the researcher rather than a fitness-influencer recap — a useful model after you practice [[A-Evidence-filter/S16-craap-test|CRAAP]] on video media. Athletes after 55 and coaches who want guideline context in plain language will benefit most. It is not a masters-only trial and still needs pairing with [[C-Programming/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]] for older-adult specificity and [[C-Programming/S02-borde-dose-response|Borde]] for dose detail. Cross-check claims against [[A-Evidence-filter/S17-adler-how-to-read|Adler]] (understand before you adopt) and deepen power methods with [[C-Programming/S19-kennerley-velocity-phd|Kennerley’s thesis]] when you want more than a conversation summary.
