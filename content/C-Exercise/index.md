# C. Exercise

**Purpose:** Give the activity-pillar spine for people who still need strength under demand: progressive resistance, dose, concurrent work, and lived practice.

This section is primer content, not a source shelf. Full citations live in [[G-Sources/index|G. Sources]].

## Outline (draft)

- Progressive resistance training still works after midlife; intensity and dose matter
- Strength and bone mineral density remain trainable with progressive loading
- Power / intent-to-move-fast belongs in the plan for function
- Concurrent lifting plus conditioning or martial arts must be designed, not guessed
- Lived practice fills gaps science rarely speaks to (mat, solo session, long teaching careers)
- Hand-off: fuel the work → [[D-Nutrition/index|Nutrition]]
