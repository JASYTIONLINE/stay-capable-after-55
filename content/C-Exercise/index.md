# C. Exercise

This section is the **activity** pillar of the triad — progressive strength and conditioning (S&C) for people who still train, plus examples from coaches and athletes who kept going. You will see dose, intensity, concurrent training, and lived practice from people who did not quit after midlife.

How should you lift, how often, how hard, and how do you mix strength with conditioning — and what do long-practicing athletes actually do?
