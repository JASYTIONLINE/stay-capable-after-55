## Citation

## Annotation

This instructional series by a competitive tai chi master targets adults 60 and older with balance, joint-friendly conditioning, and sustainable movement with athletic intent. It covers lived-example media from a professional still producing work for older athletes. It is valuable for mobility, balance, and joint longevity under training — the kind of practice that supports capacity without pretending to replace heavy strength work. Athletes protecting joints who want to train or teach late in life will benefit most. It complements resistance training (RT) and does not replace [[C-Exercise/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]] or [[A-Introduction/S01-mckendry-master-athletes|McKendry]] for strength evidence. Use beside [American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)](https://www.acsm.org) · [[E-Sleep-and-recovery/S21-acsm-recovery-older-adults|recovery note]] for active-recovery framing and [[C-Exercise/S09-christensen-solo-training-50|Christensen]] for harder martial strength and conditioning (S&C). Coming back after time off? Warm into movement via [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/index|Returning to an active lifestyle]] before you treat this as your only base.
