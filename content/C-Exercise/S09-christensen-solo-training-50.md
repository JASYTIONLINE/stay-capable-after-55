# S09 — Christensen, Solo Training 3 (2015)

**Category:** [[C-Exercise/index|C — Exercise]]
**Type:** Book

## Citation

Christensen, L. W. (2015). *Solo training 3: 50 and older*. CreateSpace Independent Publishing Platform. https://www.amazon.com/Solo-Training-Loren-W-Christensen/dp/1517332400

## Annotation

This training book for martial artists 50 and older covers solo strength and conditioning (S&C), speed and power work, and injury-aware habits written after Christensen was well past 55. It covers practice from someone who kept training and teaching into older age and wrote directly for that audience. It is valuable because lived professional experience fills a gap science reviews rarely speak to: the mat, the solo session, and the refusal to quit. Aging martial artists under skill and strength demand will benefit most. His experience is not what all older participants should expect, and the book needs more empirical backing — check hard programming claims against [[C-Exercise/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]] and dose evidence in [[C-Exercise/S02-borde-dose-response|Borde]]. Screen practice books with [Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose (CRAAP)](https://library.csuchico.edu/sites/default/files/craap-test.pdf) · [[A-Introduction/S16-craap-test|note]] and [[A-Introduction/S17-adler-how-to-read|Adler]]. If you are returning after a long layoff, do not jump to his intensity before [[B-Returning-to-active-lifestyle/index|Returning to an active lifestyle]]. Pair joint care with [[C-Exercise/S10-ross-tai-chi-fit-over-60|Ross]].
