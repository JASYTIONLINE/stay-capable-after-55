## Annotation

This Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) thesis examines maximal-intentional velocity (power-focused) resistance training design and implementation for older adults. It covers how to build and study training that emphasizes intent to move with speed — the kind of power work that [[C-Exercise/S04-fragala-nsca-older-adults|Fragala]] and current [American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)](https://www.acsm.org) guidance flag as important for function after midlife. It is valuable as deep scholarly work beyond a position-statement paragraph: methods, design choices, and older-adult application in one document. Coaches and serious athletes after 55 interested in power methods will benefit most. Thesis length and academic framing are limitations for casual readers; use selected findings, not the whole PDF as a consumer program. Screen any popular “power for seniors” video with [Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose (CRAAP)](https://library.csuchico.edu/sites/default/files/craap-test.pdf) · [[A-Introduction/S16-craap-test|note]] and [[A-Introduction/S17-adler-how-to-read|Adler]], hear the guideline layer in [[C-Exercise/S18-currier-acsm-video|Currier’s ACSM interview]], then come here when you need depth. Pair dose questions with [[C-Exercise/S02-borde-dose-response|Borde]] and recovery limits with [[E-Sleep-and-recovery/index|Sleep and recovery]].
