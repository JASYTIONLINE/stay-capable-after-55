# A. The triad and how to judge sources

**Path stop 1 of 5** — Start here if you are new to this collection.

This section frames the whole site. Army **Holistic Health and Fitness (FM 7-22)** treats sleep, activity, and nutrition as **one readiness system** — not three separate hobbies. The other tools here teach you how to judge fitness claims so you do not confuse marketing with evidence, especially for people who still train after 55.
